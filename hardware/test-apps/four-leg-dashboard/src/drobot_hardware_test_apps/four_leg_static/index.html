    REAL IMU + LIVE JOINT FEEDBACK

    RL walking policy

    Runs the selected ONNX policy at the requested speed and duration. Press PREPARE RL STANCE first; it moves all twelve joints together into the neutral pose used during training and waits for a settled hold before enabling inference. RL preserves torque through startup and returns all 12 joints to calibrated center when complete. A target-step clamp or transient deadline miss returns to center and holds; hardware faults and the explicit stop button still disarm. The 8080 process remains the only servo-bus owner and applies position, rate, tilt, telemetry, and model-output guards. Temperatures from 55–64 C are rechecked for five seconds before stopping; 65 C or higher stops immediately.
